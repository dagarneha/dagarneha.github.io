The work

ProofHub is built for teams that plan and collaborate. The design job is to make that idea obvious: what the product does, who it helps, and why a feature matters — without extra noise.

I work with product and content so campaigns, emails, and graphics use the same language. Brand guidelines keep that system usable for other teams, not only for one campaign.

  • Landing pages and web banners
  • Email and promotional layouts
  • Social creatives and campaign sets
  • Feature illustrations and product graphics
  • Brand guidelines for cross-team use

How it is designed

  1. 01

    Message before decoration

    Each asset starts with one idea — a feature, a workflow, or a campaign line — then the layout supports that, not the other way around.

  2. 02

    One system, many formats

    A campaign has to work as a banner, a social post, and an email. Type, color, and spacing stay related so the set feels like one piece.

  3. 03

    Built with the team

    Design sits next to product and content. The goal is consistent branding across digital touchpoints, not a one-off visual.

Venus Remedies

Packaging, brochures, and regulated collaterals

Graphic design for a pharmaceutical company. The work turned dense product information into packaging, brochures, and internal pieces that stay accurate and easy to follow.

Role
Graphic Designer
Client
Venus Remedies Limited
Place
Panchkula, India
Focus
Packaging & print

The work

Venus Remedies makes research-driven pharmaceuticals, including injectables. Print for that kind of product has to be precise: the name, the strength, and the use have to read before anything else.

I worked with marketing and product teams on packaging, brochures, internal communications, and social graphics — keeping brand rules and industry visual standards in place.

  • Product packaging layouts
  • Marketing brochures and print collaterals
  • Internal communication design
  • Social graphics tied to the same brand system

How it was set

  1. 01

    Accuracy first

    Copy, names, and visual standards were checked against the brand and the industry rules the team already followed.

  2. 02

    Hierarchy for small formats

    On a carton, the product name and key facts lead. Supporting detail sits where it can be read, not where it competes.

  3. 03

    One brand across channels

    Packaging, brochures, and social were designed as one system so marketing and product materials stayed consistent.

Namaste Veda & the skin-care line

Graphic design for print — labels, jars, tubes, and collaterals for a herbal cosmetic range. The brief was simple: make Ayurvedic ingredients feel clean, calm, and easy to read on a shelf.

Ultra Healthcare Official
Place

The work

At Ultra Healthcare I worked on print for personal care and cosmetics, including the Namaste Veda line — herbal beauty products such as face wash, shea body lotion, and aloe-based care. The job was to keep each pack clear: what it is, what is in it, and who it is for, without crowding the label.

  • Product labels for jars, tubes, and dropper bottles
  • Ingredient-led hierarchy — name first, benefit second
  • A shared look: cream grounds, gold caps, botanical green
  • Print-ready files for packaging and marketing collaterals

Selected packs

Cosmetic print

How the print was set

  1. 01

    One system, many SKUs

    Logo, type, and gold-and-cream packaging stayed consistent so face wash, face packs, and oils read as one range.

  2. 02

    Ingredient first

    Aloe, neem, ubtan, green tea, and rosemary sit on the front. Benefit lines stay short so the pack still feels quiet.

  3. 03

    Built for print

    Labels were laid out for tubes, jars, and bottles — clear at shelf distance, with room for net weight and usage notes.
